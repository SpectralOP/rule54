Statement inspection
The update is synchronous Rule54.cell, checked against Wolfram number 54.
initialRow depends only on the binary machine and its finite input tape.
common_tails fixes both reference backgrounds independently of machine/input;
periodic_input gives their spatial periods. The elementary-cell observer reads
the simulated control and tape, with the state count, seed length and elapsed
time as metadata. The sampler may compute timing, but returns the data read
from the actual CA trajectory. The infinite_run theorem concerns one orbit
and an increasing sequence of observation times. These are weak-universality
claims, not computation from a finite seed on a uniform background.
